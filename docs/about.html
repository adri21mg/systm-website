A little about Systm

Small studio.
Strange ideas.

Hey, I’m Adri. Systm is my independent game studio. Right now, that means me.

I’m a game developer and 3D environment artist. I’ve worked on games including Blades of Fire and Metroid Ravenous. Now I’m working toward making my own games full-time.

I like strange mechanics, uncomfortable atmospheres, and worlds worth getting lost in. Usually that takes me somewhere dark. It doesn’t always have to.

First up is TRAPPIST-1. Eventually, I’d love to build Systm into a small team of people making things we’re excited about.

Want to talk games, work together, or just say hi?
